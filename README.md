Organization Overview
ABC Carwash Services
(A division of ABC Black Group (Pty) Ltd)
Brief History
ABC Carwash Services was founded in 2024 as a side business with the initial goal of generating an additional income stream that could match the owner's full-time salary. As the business grew, the vision evolved beyond simply earning extra income.
Today, the business forms part of ABC Black Group (Pty) Ltd and is being developed into a scalable service business capable of creating employment, expanding into multiple locations, and generating long-term wealth. 
The long-term objective is for the business to acquire assets, including property, and become one of several successful businesses under the ABC Black Group umbrella.

Mission Statement
To provide convenient, professional and reliable mobile carwash services while delivering exceptional customer service and creating employment opportunities within our communities.

Vision Statement
To become one of South Africa’s most trusted mobile car wash brands through innovation, convenience, excellent customer service and sustainable business growth.
